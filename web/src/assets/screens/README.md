# Real app screenshots

Drop screenshots of the real app here and they appear on the Features page, in a gallery, with no code change.

- Formats: `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg` or `.webp`. Portrait phone screenshots look best (about 1170 x 2532).
- The file name becomes the caption: `01-recovery-score.png` shows as "Recovery score". The number sets the order.
- Use a real account with real data. Do not use sample data: the site says the app only shows real data.
- Good ones to take: Health (recovery, sleep, strain), Train (today's plan and the coach), a challenge with friends, the food scan result, the Social feed, Devices.
- On iPhone: press the side button and volume up together. Send them to your Mac with AirDrop.
